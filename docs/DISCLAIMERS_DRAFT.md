# Disclaimers (DRAFT for attorney review)

Status: draft. Not reviewed by an attorney. Shown only after review. Plain English is used on purpose. Each block names where it appears.

## A. Site-wide footer (every page)

Planet Hunter is an educational project. It shows science from public NASA and partner data and is not run by, or affiliated with, NASA or the TESS science team. Nothing on this site is an official discovery, classification or catalogue entry. Do not use it for safety, navigation or any decision that depends on being correct. The site is provided "as is", without any warranty.

## B. Candidate pages and scan results

Every signal here is a candidate: a repeating dip in brightness that might be a planet. A candidate is not a confirmed planet until professional follow-up (for example, a mass measurement or a detailed statistical check) says so. "Unreviewed" means no person has reviewed this result yet. A "batch" result and a "live" scan result can differ, and the page says which one you are looking at.

## C. AI explainer

The explainer writes a short answer from the numbers and lesson text on this site only. It can be wrong. It can say "not known" when the site does not have the answer. Check the cited fields. It does not give new scientific results, and it never calls anything a discovery. Your question and the text it works from are sent to an AI provider to produce the answer (named in the privacy notice).
